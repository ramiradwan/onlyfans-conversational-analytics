# ADR 0053: Observe deferred activation offer preparation

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-13
- Authority: product-owner approval of the deferred preparation decision and bounded nonrenewal amendment, including the reviewed warning resolutions.
- Supersedes: only ADR0051's expectation that missing original activation preparation must always wait for another explicit browser retry. That retry remains available for other eligible interruptions.

## Decision

Brain retains the accepted activation review and reconciles its original operation through the existing proof-authorized reads. Provider may finish preparation that it already accepted under the pinned [deferred setup preparation policy](../../contracts/catalog/deferred-setup-preparation-policy-v1.json). Product gains no preparation endpoint, admission authority or milestone-based eligibility decision.

While the original preparation authority remains live, generic unavailability means that preparation has not been confirmed. It does not prove ineligibility, rollback or absence of commercial effects. The native dialog uses factual copy and waits through bounded reconciliation. A prepared offer appears through the existing decision projection and still requires explicit confirmation of its exact validated terms.

Presentation or preparation expiry stops protected preparation, not contract-permitted noncreating original-outcome reconciliation. Lost or unknown outcomes remain attached to the saved operation. Committed activation delivery keeps ADR0052's separate recovery path. Brain marks Full only after verifying and persisting the issued capability license.

Product never starts another review, browser task, authentication attempt or replacement operation automatically because a read is unavailable or an old presentation expires. New reviews require explicit customer action and use fresh operation IDs. The upstream policy's bounded handling of supplied UUIDs after legitimate no-effect cleanup adds no local ID-reuse feature.

## Why

The customer has already requested the offer and completed authentication. The last approved setup fact may arrive later. An owner-held continuation can finish that same request without another browser visit, while reads remain noncreating and final commercial confirmation remains explicit.

## Consequences

Adopt the exact reviewed Contracts commit, policy document and supplemental lifecycle vectors through the existing snapshot generator. Published wire profiles, proof domains and request/response schemas remain unchanged. No compatibility adapter or legacy admission path is added.

The native UI cannot infer a pending reason from unchanged generic projections. Local milestone delivery or browser completion never proves hosted eligibility, preparation, confirmation or Full readiness. Restart and lost replies reconcile the saved scope instead of reconstructing consent.

Qualification must cover unavailable-before-offer, restart, delayed original preparation, explicit confirmation, expired or changed authority, lost original outcomes and refusal to create replacement work automatically. Installed acceptance uses real milestones and matching reviewed releases.
