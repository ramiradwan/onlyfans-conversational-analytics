# ADR 0053: Observe deferred activation offer preparation

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-13
- Authority: product-owner approval of the deferred preparation decision and bounded nonrenewal amendment, including the reviewed warning resolutions.
- Supersedes: only ADR0051's expectation that missing original activation preparation must always wait for another explicit browser retry. That retry remains available for other eligible interruptions.

## Decision

Brain retains the accepted activation review and reconciles its original operation through the existing proof-authorized reads. Provider may finish preparation that it already accepted under the pinned [deferred setup preparation policy](../../contracts/catalog/deferred-setup-preparation-policy-v1.json). Product gains no preparation endpoint, admission authority or milestone-based eligibility decision.

While the original preparation authority remains live, generic unavailability means that preparation has not been confirmed. It does not prove ineligibility, rollback or absence of commercial effects. The native dialog uses factual copy and waits through bounded reconciliation. A prepared offer appears through the existing decision projection and still requires explicit confirmation of its exact validated terms.

Presentation or preparation expiry stops protected preparation, not contract-permitted noncreating original-outcome reconciliation. Lost or unknown outcomes remain attached to the saved operation. Committed activation delivery keeps ADR0052's separate recovery path. Brain marks Full only after verifying and persisting the issued capability license.

Product never starts another review, browser task, authentication attempt or replacement operation automatically because a read is unavailable or an old presentation expires. New reviews require explicit customer action and use fresh operation IDs. The upstream policy's bounded handling of supplied UUIDs after legitimate no-effect cleanup adds no local ID-reuse feature.

## Why

The customer has already requested the offer and completed authentication. The last approved setup fact may arrive later. An owner-held continuation can finish that same request without another browser visit, while reads remain noncreating and final commercial confirmation remains explicit.

## Consequences

Adopt the exact reviewed Contracts commit, policy document and supplemental lifecycle vectors through the existing snapshot generator. Published wire profiles, proof domains and request/response schemas remain unchanged. No compatibility adapter or legacy admission path is added.

The native UI cannot infer a pending reason from unchanged generic projections. Local milestone delivery or browser completion never proves hosted eligibility, preparation, confirmation or Full readiness. Restart and lost replies reconcile the saved scope instead of reconstructing consent.

Qualification must cover unavailable-before-offer, restart, delayed original preparation, explicit confirmation, expired or changed authority, lost original outcomes and refusal to create replacement work automatically. Installed acceptance uses real milestones and matching reviewed releases.

## Expired original activation reconciliation

The published [original activation reconciliation contract](../../contracts/profiles/complimentary-activation-reconciliation/v1/profile.json) is a noncreating read. Brain reconstructs its request only from the immutable admitted activation source, retained canonical registered prepare, verified continuation ID and deadline, current installed account and original delivery reservation at the same configured Provider origin. It rechecks the same coordinates and existing installation key before the challenge, signature, protected read and local result persistence.

After the source expires, an original committed result resumes ADR0052's delivery owner with the exact existing activation operation. Existing admitted delivery recovery takes precedence and does not depend on the original source remaining available. A verified expired-uncommitted result is retained as evidence, not permission to prepare or confirm a new review. A closed refusal or unknown outcome never establishes noncommit. The original desktop operation remains pending; stale activation decisions and authentication controls are suppressed. A registered source replacement refusal can delegate only when an exact single original admitted activation is locally identifiable.

The read journal persists only original IDs, canonical request digest, attempt count, retry deadline and closed terminal observation. It admits at most three reads with durable 15/30/60-second deadlines; a closed refusal stops active reads. No new activation, approval, offer, proof envelope or redemption token is stored by this read owner. Missing or revoked source/account evidence fails closed. Terminal noncommit alone does not create a new review. The separately approved same-terms review replacement action is available only under the current authenticated creator's exact original scope and retained source evidence.

## Explicit same-terms replacement

The published [review replacement contract](../../contracts/profiles/complimentary-activation-review-replacement/v1/profile.json) requires verified expired-uncommitted evidence, an unchanged original source, activation, account and installed key, and current creator authority. Brain's read projection may expose Review offer again; it cannot reserve or send. A fresh explicit desktop command atomically retains the successor IDs and retires only the verified expired original and its obsolete child commands. The exact request is durably claimed once, with at most one fresh-proof retry of those same IDs under current actor authority. A restart without actor authority cannot send or prepare; authenticated reconciliation can continue the admitted command within its deadline. Exhausted, refused or changed evidence remains blocked rather than admitting ordinary replacement.

Only a verified prepared response can atomically adopt the reserved registered continuation and activation mapping. It inherits the original retention root; the original source is fenced. The existing commercial owner then prepares the ordinary ceremony for that exact successor. No quote, old consent, license, authentication or browser launch is carried forward. Provider must verify same terms on the new ordinary review, and the customer must explicitly confirm the actual offer. A terminal refusal or unavailable response is not proof of a new commercial outcome. Live matching-release and installed restart qualification remain required.
